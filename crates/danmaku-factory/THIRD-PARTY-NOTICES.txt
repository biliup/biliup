biliup native danmaku converter
==============================

The Rust XML-to-ASS layout and serialization algorithms are derived from
DanmakuFactory by hkm, MIT licensed, reference commit
3813e93b13b087e95901d1822baf9c3540e3f3f6.
Source: https://github.com/hihkm/DanmakuFactory

The production converter is native Rust and does not include the former
DanmakuFactory C implementation or its PCRE2 dependency.
